# Every threshold and rule records its reason

Every threshold, rule, and check states why it holds the value it holds. A published
standard with its control identifier is the strongest form, a community source with a
URL is next, and a stated judgement is the weakest form that still ships. A value with
no recorded reason does not ship.

## Consequences

Writing a check costs a citation hunt. In exchange a later reader can judge whether the
reason still holds and update the value against its source instead of guessing. Mark a
citation unverified when you did not read it in the source.
